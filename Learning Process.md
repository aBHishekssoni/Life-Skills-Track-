###   If you fill something in, explain it to someone else or write it in a way you are comfortable with. If you feel some concepts are shaky, then read them again and continue with them.

## What are active and diffused modes of thinking?
### If you want to do something important work, learning, or some new work, you work in deep focus but can not resolve it; then work for 25 minutes, then take a break of a few minutes to do something else creative, then start learning or working on that focus.

## According to the video, what are the steps to take when approaching a new topic? Only mention the points.
### - Deconstruct the skill: break it into the smallest sub-skills and practice the most important ones first.
### - Learn enough to self-correct: gather 3 to 5 resources, but use them to spot your mistakes, not to procrastinate on practising.
### - Remove barriers to practice: cut distractions (TV, phone, internet) so you actually sit down and practice.
### - Practice for at least 20 hours: pre-commit to the time, push through the early frustration, and you’ll get noticeably good.

## What are some of the actions you can take going forward to improve your learning process?
### - Don't lie to yourself.
### - Learn things in a focused way, but if you cannot get it, take a break and do something else, and then try again.
### - If you think you have completed your learning, try to explain it to others.
